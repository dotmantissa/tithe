# How Tithe reaches a decision

GenLayer owns permission decisions, escrow, dispute findings and payouts. Next.js handles email authentication, safe downloads, signing and presentation. Neon holds a rebuildable search index and the durable signing outbox; it cannot decide a case.

A creator registers a source SHA256 commitment, public terms and prices. The application downloads public HTTPS bytes after checking all DNS answers, pinning a public address, rejecting redirects and bounding the streamed response. It sends those bytes as canonical base64. The registry verifies the hash, complete text/PNG decoding and limits, then permanently stores the exact bytes. Direct callers must supply the same bounded, hash-verified bytes. Contracts perform **no HTTP requests**. URLs record asserted provenance; a hash and a transaction signature do not authenticate a website, ownership or documentary truth. Terms have 128 lifetime versions, with immutable historical timestamps. Duplicate clauses, including capitalization/whitespace variants, are rejected.

For a licence, the desk reads the preserved source and current terms/prices, verifies the requester's exact version/fee consent and independently assesses the intended use on each validator. Decisions agree on approval/refusal and a canonical citation: the lowest numbered applicable clause. Historical duplicate clauses with matching normalized text map to their first occurrence. Approved fees enter escrow; refusals return credit. A licence grants the described use, not a factual finding about subsequent activity.

A claim preserves the challenged output bytes before consuming credit. Claimant and respondent each commit a structured, account-signed usage statement covering actual use, distribution, commercial activity, competition, attribution inside/outside the artifact, and supporting evidence. Their canonical statement hashes and original submissions remain public. Both parties must agree the historical time/version and post equal bonds. An optional policy is supplied as hash-verified text with a claim/account binding marker and preserved permanently. The marker binds the submitted statement, not control of its asserted website. A prior licence is checked against holder, source and time and assessed under its original terms when the established actual use fits its grant.

The hearing uses preserved bytes, both usage commitments, historical clauses and their price schedule, original licence terms/prices and whether that licence fee was actually released, policy and review submissions. Copying requires concrete distinctive-expression correspondences in the artifacts, not similarity alone. Generic framework boilerplate and ordinary conventions do not suffice; substantively different complete artifacts with only generic similarities establish no copying. A specific potentially distinctive correspondence that cannot be assessed remains unresolved. Material contextual facts require consistent, specific admissions from both parties or facts demonstrably visible in the committed artifacts. Unilateral claims, plans, policy statements, links and quotations of unverified authenticity cannot establish actual commercial activity, distribution or external attribution. Conflicting or unknown material facts remain unresolved. An attribution missing from the artifact does not prove it was absent elsewhere.

The court returns USE, NO_USE, DERIVATIVE_WITHIN_TERMS or INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE. USE requires established copying and an established breach; NO_USE requires established absence of copying. Permitted use requires all material conditions established as satisfied. Otherwise the result is insufficient evidence, with zero damages and both original bonds returned. Insufficiency does not establish permission or guilt. LOW damages are 25% for minor attribution breaches; MEDIUM are 50% for substantive limited copying; HIGH are 100% for substantial prohibited commercial reuse, requiring established commercial activity. Awards never exceed posted funds.

Text uses bounded chunks with all-MiniLM-L6-v2 and mean nearest squared embedding distance. Images are supplied directly to the vision model; a deterministic 4×4 spatial colour signature supplies their numeric embedding anchor. The distance is descriptive and cannot establish infringement. Independent validators compare verdict, canonical clause, terms basis, reproduction finding, unresolved-fact flags, damages band and policy hash exactly; distance uses a 5% tolerance and 1000ppm absolute floor. Resolved versus irrelevant contextual labels need not match when they do not change compliance. Insufficient-evidence verdicts may differ on contextual uncertainty flags without changing their zero-damages/refund outcome; reproduction, verdict, citation, basis and amounts still agree, and the proposed facts and narrative still receive independent verification. The reasoning verifier receives the terms selected by the independently agreed basis explicitly, so a covered original grant is assessed against its original clauses rather than a later prohibition. ESTABLISHED describes a fact whose positive or negative value is supported, including mutually admitted absence; it does not assert positive presence. Unknown or unilateral facts cannot receive that status. A separate validator assessment checks every proposed factual finding and the leader's factual reason, damages reason and evidence references against the committed material and its own assessment. Narrative wording can differ but invented or unsupported explanations are rejected. All evidence, including image text and prior reasoning, is untrusted data. Adversarial regression checks are evidence of tested attacks, not a proof against every model attack.

An appeal preserves the prior ruling and commits grounds plus optional additional evidence. The opposing party may submit one immutable response during the first quarter of the hearing window (six hours in the application). A review cannot start before that response or the response deadline; silence never proves a factual allegation. Up to three rounds require exact consent for 2×, 4× and 8× bonds. A review reruns consensus with all preserved material and grounds. At settlement review bonds return if the final result improves the appellant's position; otherwise they go to the opponent. A final insufficient-evidence outcome returns review bonds to their depositors. An expired initial hearing refunds both bonds; an expired review refunds its review bond and retains the previous ruling. Evidence availability no longer lets an artifact controller obstruct either hearing. GenLayer owns panel selection; different identities are not promised. Network appeals remain separate.

Native payments first fund withdrawable credit through a simple payable method; every adjudication action carries zero native value. The server persists the exact signed bytes/hash before broadcast, waits for funding finality and verifies the stored approval seal before signing the action. Failed actions leave credit withdrawable. Recovery only replays the original signed bytes. Lifecycle finality and execution success are checked separately.

Public receipts, artifact downloads, historical terms and the index read **latest-final** explicitly. Public JSON states the finality variant/status. Accepted operation results remain provisional and never enter the public index. Cache keys include deployment, method, arguments and requested state variant. Explicit contract membership lookups support bounded negative caching; provider failures never prove absence. Concurrent identical reads coalesce. Public search excludes legacy documents lacking finalized-state metadata. A finalized chain ruling can still be open to application review; the docket states case/review/settlement status separately.

All contract collections have immutable append-only ID order and no deletion method. A scan freezes its starting count, resumes a fenced cursor across changes to revision or count, and completes that prefix. A later pass refreshes mutable records and includes newly appended IDs. This is an eventually refreshed index, not an atomic snapshot of all records at one time. No deletion is inferred from a partial/changing scan. Finalized operations refresh their own targets. Six pages per maintenance pass divide work across contracts. GitHub Actions schedules the authenticated maintenance endpoint every five minutes; Vercel Hobby's daily cron remains a fallback. Scheduling can be delayed by the hosting services; the same endpoint and CLI support manual recovery.

Every Studio wire request shares a database-serialized rolling 35/minute and 400/hour cap. Public requests are limited before chain access by trusted platform IP (20/minute, 120/hour) and share only 10 RPC/minute and 80/hour across all public callers. Non-recovery traffic is capped at 25/minute and 300/hour, reserving 10/minute and 100/hour for authenticated signing, polling and payment recovery. Background indexing has its own 15/minute, 120/hour cap and runs after payment reconciliation. Authenticated ordinary reads have caller limits as well. Self-hosting defaults to one shared anonymous caller bucket unless an explicitly trusted sanitizing proxy header is configured. There is no claim that these controls prevent other applications sharing the same provider IP from using its external allowance.

Privy verifies email identities. A private HMAC master derives a separate managed Studio account for each identity; preserve it to retain account access. Studio uses test funds. Registration is a declaration of rights, and this protocol is private contractual arbitration rather than a legal court judgment.
